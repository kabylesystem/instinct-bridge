Preparing the local connection…

Scan with your iPhone camera. The phone page guides you through the temporary certificate and Wi-Fi proxy.

The proxy accepts only Authy traffic. Other connections through it are blocked; no traffic archive is saved.

Keep Authy installed and backups enabled.

On your iPhone, turn the Wi-Fi proxy Off and remove the temporary Instinct Bridge profile in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Then enter the Authy backup password above and choose Unlock & review.

Exports and passwords stay in memory. Use “Forget data” or close the server when finished.
